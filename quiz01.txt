
SUPERCOLLIDER QUIZ 01
For a quick jogging of the memory.


-   Write a short "Hello World" program. Do it on a napkin or in the sc Interpreter.
It should simply print "Hello World". Be terse.

-   Write a hello world SC program to play a simple sine wave at 440Hz.
Be careful! What is the precondition for playing audio in SC?

-   How do you run i.e. interpret a single line? What of a block of lines?
What keyboard shortcut is convenient for this?

-   Which variable name is reserved for the server?
What are the three different ways of defining variables in SC? What distinguishes these?

-   What is a synth def in SC? How does one quickly deallocate memory for all synthdefs?
How does one deallocate a specific synthdef?
